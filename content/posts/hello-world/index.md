This is an example post. It shows everything plain Markdown gives you here, so you can see how it reads before writing anything real.

## Text

Paragraphs are just text. You get *emphasis*, **strong**, ~~strikethrough~~, `inline code`, and [links](https://gohugo.io). Footnotes work too.[^1] Typographic quotes and dashes are converted automatically: "like this" -- and this.

> A blockquote, for when someone else said it better.
> It can run over several lines.

## Lists

1. Ordered lists
2. with numbers
   - and nested
   - unordered items

- [x] Task lists
- [ ] render as checkboxes

## Images

Put images next to the post in its folder and reference them by name. The title in quotes becomes a tooltip.
